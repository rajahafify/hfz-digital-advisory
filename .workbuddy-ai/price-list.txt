10. GENERAL TERMS 
• 
All prices are in Malaysian Ringgit (RM) and are exclusive of applicable SST unless stated otherwise. 
• 
Annual hosting plans require 100% advance payment for the full 1-year period. 
• 
Annual maintenance plans require 100% advance payment for the full 1-year period. 
• 
Renewal must be paid before expiry to avoid account/service suspension. 
• 
Domain registration and hosting services are subject to the underlying provider's terms, availability and renewal 
conditions. 
• 
Promotional domain prices are subject to change and may apply only to new registrations; renewal pricing can 
differ. 
• 
Premium domains, special domain extensions and third-party software/licence fees are quoted separately. 
• 
Website projects require a confirmed scope, design approval and agreed payment schedule before 
commencement. 
• 
Recommended payment structure for website projects: 50% deposit, 40% upon development completion / 
approval, 10% before final handover. 
• 
Third-party costs such as payment gateways, Google/Meta advertising, premium plugins, themes, stock images, 
API usage and software subscriptions are excluded unless stated. 
• 
Minor revisions are subject to the package allowance. Material changes or new functionality may be quoted 
separately.
