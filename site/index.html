    Four reasons people leave your website.

    What each one costs you, and what we do instead.

    01 · The offer

    The headline says nothing.

    "Welcome to our website" tells a visitor nothing about what you sell, where you are or what it costs. They go back to Google to find out.

    Before

    Welcome to our website

    Home · About · Services · Contact

    After

    Kitchen renovation, fixed price, Cheras

    WhatsApp us

    Nielsen Norman Group: visitors leave a page in 10 to 20 seconds unless the value is clear.

    02 · The phone

    The page is not built for a phone.

    The layout is wider than the screen, so visitors pinch and zoom to read it. Most give up before they do.

    Before

    After

    Google: 53% of mobile visits are abandoned if a page takes over 3 seconds to load.

    03 · The contact path

    The only way to reach you is a form.

    A form asks for details before it gives an answer. Many people close the tab instead.

    Before

    After

    Baymard Institute: after 14 years of testing, the flow itself is often the sole cause of abandonment.

    The listing says nothing either.

    The title and description are what a searcher reads before they click. "Home | Our Company Sdn Bhd" gives them no reason to.
